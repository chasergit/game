# Audio asset credits

The following weapon audio files are stored locally in this repository. They are Opus audio copied from the OpenSpades Free Assets pak; the upstream credits identify the original sounds as CC0 / public domain.

- `rifle_fire_01.opus`, `rifle_fire_02.opus`: "The Free Firearm Sound Library" by Ben Jaszczak et al., CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
- `rifle_fire_far.opus`: same source, distant rifle layer, CC0.
- `rifle_reload.opus`, `rifle_reload_local.opus`: "Handgun reload sound effect" by zer0_sol, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/handgun-reload-sound-effect

Redistribution source and per-file license audit: https://github.com/DeathByDenim/openspades-free-pak/blob/main/License/Credits-pak001-Free.md
